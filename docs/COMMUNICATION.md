# Communication

Written 28 October 2026. How idansegev.com gets talked about outside the site: the content strategy, the voice, and the launch post. The strategy was set in a claude.ai thread and recalled on 18 October 2026 in "Defining taste for a LinkedIn post"; this file is now the reference.

## Strategy

**Pillars.** Taste, building, leadership. Every piece of content is judged against all three.

**Positioning.**
- Theme: design leader and builder.
- Brand promise: building teams of builders.
- Core belief: building, leadership and taste together are the game changer.
- Watch-out: diluting the leadership brand. The site shows a leader who builds, not a hobbyist.

**Channels.**

| Channel | Job |
|---|---|
| Website | Holds the code and the live experiences. Everything points here. |
| Instagram, X | Drive traffic, build reputation. |
| LinkedIn | Channels the work to the relevant audience: product and exec leaders. |

**Approach: giving tools.**
- Share the work with copyable prompts people can drop into their own AI.
- No asking for tags. No showcasing remixes.
- The team's builds stay private for now.

## Voice

Learned from the drafts on 18 and 28 October 2026.

- **First person, close, personal.** "What it means for me", not "what it is". Tell it as Idan's experience.
- **Not full of yourself.** Cut lines like "Taste decides what ships" and "This is the skill I want every designer on my team to build". Leadership comes through quietly, in how you think out loud.
- **Admit you are still learning.** "Right now I think", "I'm still learning".
- **Low-pressure offers.** "If you feel like playing", not "Copy it, change it, make it yours".
- **No apology.** "Never had the time", not "couldn't afford committing to it".
- **Specific over general.** Real details (what, where, when) make it closer.
- **Restraint, like the site.** Short lines, no hype words, no emoji walls.

## Recurring ideas

- **Taste** = creativity (I can try anything), restraint (I pick one), focus (I stay with it until it feels done).
- **AI made the first part easy.** The other two still take practice.
- **Taste-maxing:** making a lot, keeping a little. Use it lightly ("a little"), never as a slogan. If it sticks, it can become a hashtag or a section title, so every channel carries the same phrase.
- **The fun came back.** Loved creative coding, never had the time, AI made it fun again.

Posting notes:
- On LinkedIn the link goes in the first comment for reach. Keep 3 hashtags or fewer.
- The share preview is the poster. Share a `/p/<slug>/` link when you want a specific poster in the preview.
- Once the site shows each poster's prompt, add back "Each one has its prompt, if you feel like playing." That line delivers the giving-tools pillar. As of 28 October 2026 the site has no prompts yet, so the line is out.

## Before posting anything

1. Does it touch taste, building and leadership, or at least not undercut one?
2. Would it read as full of yourself? Take the loudest line out.
3. Does it send people to the site?
4. Does it ask for tags or show other people's remixes? Remove that.
5. Does it show team work that is still private? Remove that.
