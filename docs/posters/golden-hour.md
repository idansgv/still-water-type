# Golden hour (a light study)

**Brief (Idan, 17 Oct 2026).** "Let's get back to volumetric. I want to double down on realistic lighting. We can let go of the letters for a minute and test a light coming through a shape (now square, later letters) and lighting dust holding in mid air, and an open window, breezes softly. Think warm, analogue Friday afternoon. Lazy vibe. Golden hour lighting." Follows Glint (parked for refinement).

**Test page:** source `docs/posters/prototypes/golden-hour.html` (WebGL2, no library). A room ray-marched in one fragment shader: low sun through a window in the left wall; the light that reaches any point is found by tracing toward the sun to the window and reading a mask there (panes, open square, or the name cut out), softened with distance (mipmapped mask, a cheap penumbra). The air scatters it (Henyey-Greenstein, forward), with slow breeze-blown haze. Floorboards and plaster, a warm bounce from the bright patch. A sheer curtain sways with lazy gusts and breathes the light. 2,600 dust motes drift in the warm air, carried by the breeze, catching the light only inside the beam, soft bokeh when near or far. Film: exposure, warm grade, vignette, grain, a slow handheld drift. Pointer: the camera leans toward it, and moving it through the beam stirs the dust.
