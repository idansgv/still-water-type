# Garden (proposed, not built)

**Status:** proposed on 5 October 2026 as a fourth poster after Skyline, Collapse and Explode. Idan: "still wanted, as a fourth", strictly black and white. No code exists.

**Reference.** Type Garden (type-garden.vercel.app): typing a word grows vines, leaves and roses out of the letters in flat colour (generative illustration, with PNG and SVG export). Our version has to be its own thing inside the site's rules: black and white, no typing UI, no instruction text, physics always.

**The idea.** Plain "Idan Segev" at rest. Touching a letter plants a seed. A vine grows from it as a physical rope that sways with the finger as wind, slides around the letters instead of through them (collision with the type mask), and weaves over and under the type with a knocked-out edge. A hold grows it longer, ending in an open spiral bloom (a rose, drawn as a spiral). A tap on a bloom drops its petals, which flutter down with drag. All line work, no colour.

**Open decisions.** How the vines collide with the type; how a plant is limited (so a page does not fill); whether the type itself is affected; how it resets; cost on phones (rope physics plus petals are new code).
