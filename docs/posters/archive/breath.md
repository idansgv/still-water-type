# Breath ("Clear.")

**Status:** removed at Idan's request on 5 October 2026 (it was a draft, then briefly live). Code: git history, last present at commit `e09f0ad` (`src/posters/breath.js`).

**Concept.** Frosted glass with "Clear." behind it. Wipe it clear and the fog finds its way back. Idan's first verdict: "has potential".

**At rest.** Dark frosted glass with the word behind it, soft and legible.

**Underneath.** A fog field that regrows from its neighbours, read through a fixed grain map so it breaks into patches rather than a smooth gradient.

**Interactions.** Drag to wipe a clear path; hover wipes lightly; the fog closes back from the edges and specks last longest.

**Why it went.** Left for the shuffle to be tightened to Still Water, Sheet and Soft type. It was one of the "physical material behind the type" ideas that landed, and could be revisited (the word, the wipe edge and the fog speed were the open items).
