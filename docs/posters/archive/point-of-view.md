# Point of view

**Status:** removed on 5 October 2026 (a draft, briefly live in Release 1, that Idan judged below the bar). Code: git history, last at commit `e09f0ad` (`src/posters/point-of-view.js`).

**Concept.** A drifting cloud of points that nearly reads "POINT OF VIEW" and resolves only from one secret viewpoint: an anamorphosis.

**Underneath.** 20,000 to 40,000 points placed on sight lines from a single hidden viewpoint (random per load), so the word appears only from that angle.

**Interactions.** Move or drag to look around and find the angle.

**Why it went.** Idan's verdict was that the stipple read as noise and did not reach Still Water's bar. The idea of a flat image that is secretly 3D and revealed by moving the view lives on in Sheet and Skyline.
