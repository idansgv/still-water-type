# Rigged

**Status:** published 21 Oct 2026 (`src/posters/rigged.js`, `?p=rigged`). The test page `docs/posters/prototypes/rigged.html` stays as the tuning bench.

**Brief (Idan, 19 Oct 2026).** The name in one of the non-default faces we have used, as cloth: shaped and held in place by a hidden, intricate set of pulleys, strings and axes laid out in a 2D plane, pulling the cloth perfectly into the letters. Use a physics engine like the other posters. Added mid-build: "think of Leonardo da Vinci mechanical sketches" (his folio of pulleys, hanging weights and quarter-circle construction arcs).

**Idea.** Every letter is a piece of cloth cut a little too big (slack). Along its outline, strings run out to pulleys, over them, and down to small counterweights; each string has a stop, so the weights pull the edge exactly onto the letter while the spare cloth folds in the middle. Two strings per letter are nails at the top and are never cut. Nothing of the rig is seen; the cloth just looks impossibly held.

**The moment.** Tap a letter and its strings are snipped: the cloth flops, hanging from its two nails, the weights drop away. A beat later the rig reels it back up, string by string, into the letter. Double tap drops the whole name; three quick taps reveal the rig as a da Vinci study (hairline pulleys on brackets, teardrop weights, dashed arcs, italic letter labels). Hold or drag pulls the cloth against the weights. Self-demo: the page opens with the name hanging and hoists it once.

**Test page:** `docs/posters/prototypes/rigged.html` (WebGL cloth, own position-based solver in 3D so folds buckle out of the page; 2D canvas for the drawing). Faces: Bungee (default, from Slinky), Bungee Inline, Dela Gothic One, Bowlby One, Rubik Mono One, Alfa Slab One (all from Fontapalloza). Settings: face, slack, cloth detail, string spacing, fold stiffness, gravity, reel speed, rig visibility (hidden, while working, always), what a tap drops.

**Split (Idan, 19 Oct 2026).** "I love the cloth behaviour when detached from the strings and dynamically hanging, maybe it's worth splitting: one poster should maximise cloth behaviour including tear; the second should optimise the pulleys and ropes rig so that we have a more thoughtful, elegant application: fewer elements, more carefully placed." The cloth poster is now Torn (`torn.md`). Round 1 is kept as `prototypes/rigged-v1.html`.

**Round 2 (v2, same test link).** Only each letter's outermost top and bottom points and its sharp convex corners are rigged, and every rope runs straight up or straight down. A corner facing up is hoisted: its rope rises to a pulley on a rail above the line and drops to a counterweight resting on a small ledge, which holds the corner exactly; pull the cloth and the weights lift off their ledges. A corner facing down carries a plumb bob that swings (only where it hangs clear of the letter). The rig is drawn behind the cloth, so a rope running behind an arm vanishes. The cloth is stiffer (sailcloth) so few points hold the letter. About 55 parts in all, against ~230 strings in round 1. Tap cuts the nearest rope (it is tied again and reeled in after a pause); double tap ties all now; three taps invert the page and draw the hidden parts of the ropes, dashed. Open: a very hard pull still bounces a letter up towards the rail before it settles; a pair of pulleys sometimes sits almost on top of each other.

**Round 2 tuning (Idan, 21 Oct 2026).** The rig is drawn pale, like the hidden parts of other posters (Slinky's ledge, Rain's sides): hairlines at `tone` (ink strength), weights filled with a faint tint; the reveal draws it at full strength. His defaults: Alfa Slab One, counterweight 2.25, plumb 0.075, slack 0.97, corner 0.95, fold stiffness 0.26, gravity 1400, reel 240, pause 1.6 s, detail 4700, fold light 0.1, rig tone 0.15.

**Hidden battens (21 Oct 2026).** With Idan's tuned values the letters bent (few rigged corners, pre-tensioned cloth). Each letter now has long, weak ties across it between points 0.3 to 0.75 cap heights apart, like battens in a sail: the letter keeps its shape between the rigged corners while the cloth still folds and swings. Setting "Hidden battens", default 0.25 (0 turns them off). About 4 ms a frame at 1280 x 800. The test page also has test-only Page (system, light, dark) and Colour pass (the shell's six palettes, same blend modes) switches, and a viewport meta for phones.
