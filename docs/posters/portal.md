# Portal type (test)

**Brief (Idan, 17 Oct 2026).** Picked from five ideas after the PlayCanvas v2.23.0 release ("let's try 4"): the letters are windows cut through the page; through them, a different black-and-white world; the page stays plain; dragging slides the world behind the glass.

**Test page:** source `docs/posters/prototypes/portal.html` (Canvas 2D, no library). The world behind is the other colour (night through a white page, day through a black one): deep stars flying toward you, rain, or a floor of lines to a horizon. Your pointer is your eye: the world shifts with it (parallax) and the page shows its thickness, the inside walls of the cut letters, on the far side. A drag slides the world and lets it glide on. On a phone, tilting is the eye where the browser allows it. Settings: the world, wall thickness, how much it follows the eye, speed, colour easter egg.
