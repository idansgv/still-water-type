# Brandbook

**Status:** draft poster (24 Oct 2026): `src/posters/brandbook.js`, `?p=brandbook` (settings: `&tune`). The Artifact test page stays as the reference: `docs/posters/prototypes/brandbook.html`, https://claude.ai/artifact/GqMCi8hGVLzm6zg2BFg26W.

**Brief (Idan, 24 Oct 2026).** A fake black-and-white brandbook: each refresh shows a new "brand", inspired by other things. The inspiration stays invisible (no source credit on the page).

**Idea.** The brand is always "Idan Segev": every showing rebrands the name. Each brand is rolled from a *school*, a set of rules lifted from a world (transit signage, a seal-stamped shop, a 70s agency manual, a photocopied zine, a design school's primer, a letterpress jobbing printer, a classified dossier, high-contrast modern fashion). Only the rules travel (grid, case, tracking, alignment, mark construction, rule weights, how white space behaves, a voice line); never a source's marks or faces. Within a school, the roll varies the mark, the volume number, the greys and which don'ts are shown, so no two showings match. Never the same school twice in a row.

**The spread.** One open book: left page the cover (lockup, mark, "Brand guidelines", volume), right page the system (mark with clear space, type specimen, the grey palette with the school's own names, the grid, the voice line, three don'ts). Black, white and greys only.

**The moment (proposed, not agreed).** Hold: the brand doesn't cut, it morphs into the next one (type swaps glyph by glyph, the grid slides, the mark redraws). Plus live don'ts: drag the mark on the misuse page and it does exactly what the rule forbids, let go and it snaps back. Three taps: reveal (open; maybe the construction lines of the mark and grid).

**Inspiration rule (from amirmushichge/brand-system-skill, CC BY 4.0, concept only).** Separate *transferable principles* from *reference-specific elements*: take the rule, never the look.

**Open.** Which surprise; whether the site version is one spread or page-turnable; canvas port (tests sample the stage canvas).

**v2 (24 Oct 2026, same link).** Idan: v1 was "extremely basic", not as creative as Amir's examples. Diagnosis against Amir's Juicee board and a Pinterest pass: no concept, no signature device, no scale contrast, one spread instead of many pages, no applications, flat vector. v2 is a wall of 12 16:9 slides (cover, idea, contents, logo, misuse, typography, tone, device, imagery, in use x2, end). Each roll picks a venture named after him (Records, Swimming Club, Hardware, Observatory, Bakery, Radio 88.4, Transit, Type Foundry, Boxing Gym, Hotel), one of 14 display faces with its own case/tracking, and a device = field (sphere, letter or monogram, waves, burst, rings) x renderer (halftone, engraved lines, ordered dither, contour bands, coarse bitmap), drawn on canvas. Section titles are fitted to ~118% of the slide and cropped at a rolled corner. v1 kept as `prototypes/brandbook-v1.html`.

**v3: hold to morph (24 Oct 2026, same link).** Idan: "The look works. Now build the surprise: hold morphs this brand into the next one." The next brand (never the same venture or face) is built in the background as an overlay inside each slide. Hold anywhere (250 ms, 8 px slop) runs t from 0 to 1 over 2.2 s; every effect is a pure function of t, so letting go before halfway rewinds and after halfway finishes. Per slide, with a delay rippling out from the finger: each display letter switches to the new face at its own random moment; the device fields melt from the old shape into the new one (old renderer, coarse while moving); then the new brand shows through a mask of its own screen (dots that grow for halftone/dither/bitmap, lines that thicken for line/contour). At t = 1 the overlays are promoted in place and the next brand starts preparing. Self-demo: 2.6 s after load, with no touch, it morphs a third of the way and back, once (off under reduced motion). Test-only: `__setT(v)` steps the morph.

**v4: live don'ts (24 Oct 2026, same link).** Idan: "The morph feels right. Next: live don'ts on the misuse slide." Each misuse tile shows its rule broken at rest. Drag the lockup and you break it further by hand (stretch follows x, squash follows y, tilt turns with the drag, fade with distance, the shadow follows the finger, the outline hollows out, crowd pushes it into the corner and grows it). Let go and a spring (k 260, damping 15, a little overshoot) snaps it back into the correct lockup; the cross fades out and the caption becomes "Better." / "Thank you." / "Much better." / "That's the one." It stays correct until dragged again. Tiles never start the hold-to-morph.

**v5: three-tap reveal (24 Oct 2026, same link).** Idan: "Build the three-tap reveal: show the construction lines of the mark and grid." Three quick taps (each under 250 ms and 8 px, gaps under 380 ms) draw hairline construction over every slide, in difference blend so it reads on black, white and grey: the 12-column grid with margins (labelled with gutter and margin in px); baselines, cap heights and x-heights of the big display lines (labelled "baseline", "cap 0.xx em"); for each mark its box, centre cross, inscribed circles, diagonals and a dashed clear-space square; for each device the geometry its field is built from (sphere radius and light direction, burst circles and rays, the 8 ring bands, the letter's square at 86%, the wave curve). Lines draw in with staggered delays; three taps again fade them out. A hold hides them; a morph or new brand clears them.

**Port to the site (24 Oct 2026).** Idan: "port Brandbook into the site as a draft poster." The site's tests read the poster canvas, so it is a rebuild in 2D canvas rather than a copy: each slide is painted into its own offscreen canvas (cached; repainted only while it morphs or a misuse tile moves), the stage draws the visible ones. Same brand roll, fields and screens as v5; the misuse lockups, the morph (glyph by glyph, device melt, reveal through the next brand's own dots or lines) and the construction drawing all work from what the painter records (baselines, cap heights, marks, devices, tiles). The construction is the engine's reveal (three taps, page inverts). Asked how the twelve slides should sit on one screen, Idan said "put the variations in the settings": Layout 0 a wall you scroll inside the poster (wheel, drag with a little inertia; one column under 760 px), 1 the whole book on one screen (cover 2 x 2 cells, the rest one each, the column count with the biggest cells wins), 2 one slide at a time (wheel, swipe or arrow keys). The desk follows the theme (#ececec on light, #121212 on dark). Fonts are requested from Google Fonts per brand (one stylesheet per family, removed on destroy). Self-demo: a third of a morph and back, once, 2 s after the next brand is ready, if untouched. Open: which layout is the default; phone not looked at; the cover's device can sit over the meta row.

**Whole book, click to turn (24 Oct 2026).** Idan: "1 (whole book) is preferable. let's have a click to an animated transition to the next one." Layout 1 is now the default (the other two stay in the settings). A tap plays the morph through to the next brand (1.7 s, rippling out from the tap); it waits 420 ms first, the same beat as a double tap's re-form, so the first of three quick taps (reveal) never starts it. Hold still scrubs it by hand. The self-demo now rewinds at the first touch instead of stopping where it was.
